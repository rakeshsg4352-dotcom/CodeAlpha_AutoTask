📋 Example Input Format

AutoTask automatically parses unstructured paragraphs, email headers, log entries, and lists:

Hello Team,
Contact us at support@example.com.
For enterprise sales, write to sales@example.org.
Our administrator email is admin@example.com.
Feel free to email support@example.com again anytime.

⚙️ Algorithm Features

  • Regex Pattern Recognition: Uses RFC-compatible pattern matching (re module).
  • Order Preservation: Duplicate occurrences (e.g. repeated support@example.com) are stripped while strictly maintaining first-seen order.
  • Punctuation Trimming: Trailing sentence punctuation (dots, commas) is cleanly stripped from addresses.
  • Export & Archival: Generates a clean text file in your media storage, ready to download instantly.
